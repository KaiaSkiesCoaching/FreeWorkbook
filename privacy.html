Datenschutzerklärung

Last updated: June 2026 — GDPR compliant

Zuletzt aktualisiert: Juni 2026 — DSGVO-konform

Your privacy matters. We only use analytics to understand how we can improve your experience. We do not sell your data, and we only activate tracking after you give explicit consent.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for this website is Kaia Skies. For contact details, please refer to our Legal Notice.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data when you visit this website:

  • Analytics data (only with your consent): page views, session duration, device type, approximate location (country/region), traffic source — collected via Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation enabled.
  • Cookie consent preference: stored locally in your browser via localStorage — never transmitted to our servers.

We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifying information directly through this website.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our website. This service is operated by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Google Analytics is only activated after you explicitly accept cookies. When active, it uses cookies to collect anonymised usage data. IP anonymisation is enabled, meaning your full IP address is never stored.

You can opt out at any time by revisiting this site and declining cookies, or by using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Our legal basis for processing analytics data is your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw this consent at any time by declining cookies. No data is processed before consent is given.

5. Cookies

  • _ga, _ga_XXXXXXXX — Google Analytics session and user identification (expires after 2 years). Only set after consent.
  • kaia_cookie_consent — Stores your cookie preference locally in your browser (localStorage). Not a cookie; not transmitted.

6. Third-Party Services & Email Notifications

The free workbook is delivered via Systeme.io. When you click the download link, you will be redirected to their platform, where you provide your email address to receive the workbook. Systeme.io has its own privacy policy which governs data collected there.

By requesting the free workbook, you will also receive email updates about the upcoming paid Complete Edition of "The Fearless Flyer" — including the launch notification and the special launch price for subscribers. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email.

7. Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default, after which it is automatically deleted. We do not retain any other personal data.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact us at crew@kaiaskies.com or via the details in our Legal Notice.
